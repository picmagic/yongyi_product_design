What are Coins?

Coins are reward records earned through the Deeplove Creator or Referrer Reward Program. Diamonds are used for in-app purchases, while Coins may be used to apply for a USD payout. They cannot be used interchangeably.

Exchange Rate

1,000 Coins = 1 USD.

How do I earn Coins?

You may earn Coins when a new user joins Deeplove through your invitation and uses Diamonds they purchased. Rewards are calculated under the applicable program rules.

What activity is not eligible?

Promotional, subscription-bonus, compensation Diamonds, and transactions linked to refunds, chargebacks, or abnormal activity are not eligible for rewards.

How do I exchange Coins?

Confirm your available Coin balance.

Enter the amount and payout information.

Submit your request for review.

Approved requests will be processed based on the amount shown on the page.

Important Notes

Only Coins shown as “Available” can be exchanged. Pending Coins become available after the applicable order review period.

The minimum exchange amount, available payout methods, and supported regions are shown on the application page.

Coins are not legal tender, deposits, or investment products. They may only be exchanged under the Deeplove Reward Program.

Deeplove may cancel rewards or reject exchange requests involving fraudulent invitations, abnormal registrations, manipulation, refunds, chargebacks, or other violations.

Final approval and payout are subject to system review and applicable legal requirements.
